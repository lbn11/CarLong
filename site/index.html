About ads and your data

关于广告与你的数据

The game is free and supported by rewarded ads: you choose when to watch one, and you get the reward only after watching it through. Ads are served by Google AdMob. There is no account and no sign-up — your progress stays on your device.

游戏免费，靠激励视频广告支撑：看与不看由你决定，并且只有看完才会发奖。 广告由 Google AdMob 提供。游戏没有账号体系、不需要注册，进度只存在你自己的设备上。

What the ad SDK receives, how to turn off personalised ads, and how to withdraw consent are all described in the Privacy Policy.

广告 SDK 会收到哪些信息、如何关闭个性化广告、如何撤回同意，都写在 隐私政策里。
