Privacy Policy

Game: Merge Fleet (车水马龙) · Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Effective: 23 October 2026

The short version. There is no account and no sign-up. Your game progress never leaves your device. The only third party that receives data is our advertising provider, Google AdMob, and only for the purpose of showing ads. You can turn personalised ads off at any time.

1. Who we are

Merge Fleet ("the Game", "we", "us") is a mobile game published for iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play) by an independent developer, who is the data controller for the purposes described below. You can reach us at lbn19851202@gmail.com.

2. What we do not collect

We do not ask for, and do not have access to, your name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise GPS location, or any health data. The Game has no account system, no login, and no user-generated content. The Game does not include any analytics SDK.

3. Information handled when you play

3.1 Stored on your device only

Game progress (coins, unlocked vehicles, unlocked levels, daily counters, sound and vibration preferences) is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. Deleting the Game, or clearing its data, permanently removes it. Because we never receive it, we cannot restore it.

3.2 Collected by our advertising partner

Ads are served by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the advertising identifier of your device (IDFA on iOS, Android Advertising ID on Android), together with its "limit ad tracking" / "delete advertising ID" state;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (country or city level) is derived — we never request precise location;
  • device and app information: device model, operating system version, system language, screen size, app version and package name;
  • ad interaction events: that an ad was requested, filled, displayed, clicked, or completed, plus technical diagnostic and anti-fraud signals.

Google acts as an independent controller for some of this processing. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Why this information is used

  • to display rewarded video ads, which is how the Game stays free;
  • to cap how often the same ad is shown to you;
  • to detect and prevent invalid traffic and ad fraud (this protects both you and us);
  • to measure ad performance in aggregate (fill rate, completion rate, revenue);
  • to comply with legal obligations and enforce our policies.

Advertisements in the Game are rewarded, opt-in ads only. We do not show interstitial ads during gameplay, and we never grant a reward for an ad you did not finish watching.

5. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where the GDPR or equivalent law applies, we rely on:

ProcessingLegal basis
Use of the advertising identifier and personalised advertising Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), collected through the consent form you are shown on first launch and stored on your device
Non-personalised advertising, frequency capping, fraud prevention, security Our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in funding the Game and keeping advertising honest
Responding to your support request Your request itself, and our legitimate interest in answering it

You may withdraw consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information for money. The only recipient of the data described above is Google (as our advertising provider), and only for the purposes in section 4. In the limited situations described below, data may also be disclosed:

  • to comply with a legal obligation, court order or lawful request;
  • to protect the rights, safety or property of users or of us;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy would continue to apply until updated.

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended (CCPA/CPRA), the use of an advertising identifier for cross-context behavioural advertising may be considered "sharing". If you are a California resident and you have consented to personalised ads, you can opt out at any time using the in-game control described in section 7.

7. Your choices and rights

7.1 Turning off personalised ads

  • In the Game: open Settings (gear icon on the home screen) → Privacy settings and ad preferences. This reopens the consent form, where you can change or withdraw your choices. It is always available, including after you have answered once.
  • On iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" or the switch for this app. You can also reset the identifier under Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • On Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalisation.

Turning personalisation off does not remove ads. It means the ads you see are selected without using your advertising identifier for personalisation.

7.2 Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because the Game has no account, we hold no record that identifies you. If you contact us, we can only act on data that you provide in that message. For requests concerning data held by Google as an advertising provider, please use your Google account controls or contact Google directly; we will assist where we can.

8. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and the UK), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The Game is not listed in a "kids" category on either store, and ad requests are not tagged as child-directed. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action, including deleting any data we hold.

9. Retention

  • Game progress: stored on your device until you delete the Game or clear its data.
  • Consent record: stored on your device together with a timestamp, so that we do not ask you again unnecessarily; cleared when you clear the app's data.
  • Ad-related data: retained by Google in accordance with Google's own retention policies; we do not hold a copy.
  • Support emails: kept only as long as needed to resolve the issue, then deleted.

10. International transfers

Google may process advertising data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, such transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard. Details are available in Google's privacy documentation.

11. Security

Game data is kept in the app's sandboxed storage. Network requests made by the advertising SDK use HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we keep the data we handle to the minimum described above, which limits the impact of any incident.

12. Links to other sites

This website links to Google's privacy pages. We are not responsible for the content or practices of third-party sites; please review their policies.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if we add a feature that changes what is processed. The "Last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version. Material changes will be announced in the Game or on this page before they take effect. Continuing to play after an update means you accept the updated policy.

14. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints about privacy: lbn19851202@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 3 business days, and within one month for formal rights requests.

隐私政策

一句话版本。 游戏没有账号、不需要注册，你的进度从不离开你的设备。 唯一会收到数据的是广告服务商 Google AdMob，且只用于展示广告。个性化广告随时可以关闭。

1. 我们是谁

车水马龙（Merge Fleet，"本游戏"）是一款由独立开发者发布在 iOS（App Store）与 Android（Google Play）上的手机游戏。该开发者是本政策所述处理活动的数据控制者。 联系方式：lbn19851202@gmail.com。

2. 我们不收集什么

我们不会索要、也无法获取你的姓名、电子邮箱、手机号、住址、通讯录、照片、相机、 麦克风、精确 GPS 位置或任何健康数据。游戏没有账号体系、没有登录、没有用户生成内容， 也没有集成任何数据分析（analytics）SDK。

3. 游戏过程中涉及的信息

3.1 仅保存在你设备上的信息

游戏进度（金币、已解锁车型、已解锁关卡、每日计数、音效与振动偏好）保存在你设备上 本应用的私有存储中，不会传输给我们或任何第三方。删除游戏或清除应用数据后，这些内容 会被永久删除。由于我们从不接收这些数据，因此无法为你恢复。

3.2 广告服务商会收集的信息

广告由 Google AdMob 提供。在请求广告时，Google Mobile Ads SDK 可能会收集并处理：

  • 设备的广告标识符（iOS 为 IDFA，Android 为 Android 广告 ID）， 以及"限制广告跟踪"／"删除广告 ID"的状态；
  • 你的 IP 地址，由此推导出大致位置（国家或城市级）—— 我们从不请求精确位置；
  • 设备与应用信息：设备型号、系统版本、系统语言、屏幕尺寸、应用版本与包名；
  • 广告交互事件：广告的请求、填充、展示、点击、完播，以及技术诊断与反作弊信号。

对于其中部分处理，Google 作为独立的控制者。详见 Google 隐私权政策 与 Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的网站或应用的信息。

  • 限制同一广告对你的展示频次；
  • 识别并阻止无效流量与广告作弊（这既保护你也保护我们）；
  • 以汇总形式衡量广告效果（填充率、完播率、收入）；
  • 履行法律义务、执行我们的政策。

游戏内广告只有激励视频，且必须由你主动点击。我们不在游戏过程中插入插屏广告， 也不会为没看完的广告发放奖励。

  • 为遵守法律义务、法院命令或合法要求；
