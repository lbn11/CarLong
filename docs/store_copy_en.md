## 完整描述 / Full Description（≤4000 字符，App Store 与 Google Play 通用）

Merge Fleet (车水马龙) is a free traffic-themed puzzle game with two ways to play, sharing one garage.

MERGE MODE
Drag vehicles onto the board and merge two of the same tier to build the next one up. Climb a 50-tier ladder — from a humble delivery van all the way to a maglev train and stranger things at the top. Every merge reveals a new, hand-crafted vehicle. Play endless runs or take on daily challenges.

PARKING MODE
A slide-and-solve parking puzzle: free the blocked car in as few moves as possible. Levels are generated and hand-tuned so every board is solvable, with a difficulty curve that breathes instead of spiking.

BOSS CHAPTERS
In special chapters the boss doesn't just sit there — it moves on your board and interferes with your merging. Read its tells, plan around the chaos, and come out on top.

FREE TO PLAY, FAIR WITH ADS
The game is free and supported by rewarded video ads. You choose when to watch one, and you only get the reward after watching it all the way through. No forced ads, no account, no sign-up. Your progress is saved locally on your device.

FEATURES
• 50 vehicle tiers to discover and collect
• Two game modes: endless merging and parking puzzles
• Daily challenges and an all-levels progression
• Boss chapters with a boss that actually plays against you
• Calm, juicy visuals and satisfying merge feedback
• No account required — pick up and play

Privacy-first: the app collects only what is needed to serve ads and has no account system. See the in-game Privacy section or our Privacy Policy (carlong.pages.dev/privacy) for full details.
